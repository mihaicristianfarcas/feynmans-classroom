# Competitors: who else gets the hours and the money

Checked 9 November 2026. Labels: **[primary]** the vendor's own page; **[secondary]** press,
aggregator or review site (several are written by competitors, noted where so).

## Takeaway

Nobody sells our combination, but every part of it is sold or given away somewhere. Interview-prep
companies own the deadline money and the question banks, and they are adding AI tutors and AI mock
interviewers. Publishers own the books and are putting AI inside them. Google, OpenAI, Anthropic
and Amazon give "explain this passage" away. Spaced-repetition tools own retention but make you
write the cards. The open slot is narrow and real: **the book you already own, drawn as systems,
explained back, and kept**, priced below the interview-prep band and running on the user's own AI
plan.

## 2. Interview prep: owns the deadline money

**Hello Interview** [primary, pricing page]: 43 premium videos, 66 "Guided Practice" problems, an
**AI tutor**, 5,000+ candidate-reported questions, and **live AI-interviewer** sessions for system
design (1 a month, 2 a year, 10 lifetime). Claims calibration on "10,000+ human-led mock
interviews". Live human mocks appear to have been discontinued [secondary,
[PracHub](https://prachub.com/resources/hellointerview-mock-interviews-are-gone-the-best-alternatives-in-2026), a competitor].
Funding about **$5.9M**, 11–51 staff [secondary,
[Seedtable](https://prod.actual.seedtable.com/startups/hello-hinterview), undated]. Guided Practice
"identifies missing steps" with immediate AI feedback [secondary,
[review](https://kitemetric.com/blogs/is-hello-interview-worth-it-a-developer-s-honest-review)].
*The closest feature to our explain-back.*

**ByteByteGo** (Alex Xu, Sahn Lam) [primary, site]: a 30-chapter system-design course in text and
illustrations, preview chapters, no refunds on courses. Claims "over 5 million people", 1M+ YouTube
subscribers, 4,000+ book reviews at 4.6/5. Newsletter: **1.35M+** subscribers, 35%+ open rate, sells
sponsorships [primary,
[sponsorship page](https://blog.bytebytego.com/p/newsletter-sponsorships-471)]. No AI tutor, mock or
spaced repetition mentioned. *An author-led brand built on books: a model partner, not just a rival.*

**DesignGurus** ("Grokking") [primary, pricing page]: monthly $123, yearly $373 with 5 AI *coding*
mock interviews, one-off roadmaps ($331 coding, $345 system design), lifetime all-courses $1,238
with 7 AI mocks. No AI tutor or spaced repetition. Prices labelled regional.

**Aced, formerly Exponent**: rebranded in 2026 with an AI coach, "Ace", and a higher-priced Expert
tier [primary, [Exponent blog](https://www.tryexponent.com/blog/exponent-is-becoming-aced);
secondary, [techinterview.org](https://www.techinterview.org/bootcamp_directory/p/aced/)]. Its claims
("500K+ candidates") are unaudited [secondary]. Peer mock interviews.

**NeetCode Pro** [primary]: 200+ videos including system design, 300+ problems, "NeetBot" chat, AI
hints. No mocks or spaced repetition. **LeetCode Premium**: company-tagged lists, priority judging
[secondary]. **interviewing.io**: anonymous human mocks with engineers from big companies, from
~$179 a session [secondary].

**What the group can't do:** work from the book on your desk; schedule what you will forget; grade
*your* explanation against *the source*. **What they will do next:** more AI practice. Hello
Interview already has the tutor and missing-step feedback. **Our stance:** complement, don't fight.
Engineers use DDIA *and* a question bank. A partnership (their questions, our reading loop) is
more plausible than winning on question banks.

## 3. Technical libraries: own the books

**O'Reilly Learning**: individual **$49/month or $499/year**, 10-day trial; 60,000+ books and
videos and the **O'Reilly Answers** AI chatbot; teams $499 per member a year [secondary,
[DEV](https://dev.to/royce_fabbd83cb268312e928/oreilly-learning-platform-review-2026-pricing-and-value-2oab),
[Vendr](https://www.vendr.com/buyer-guides/oreilly)]. O'Reilly publishes DDIA.

**Manning**: subscriptions $19.99/month or $199.99/year (lite); print books include the eBook and
**liveBook with an AI assistant** that answers questions "in any language" [primary,
[subscriptions](https://www.manning.com/subscriptions); secondary,
[bookseller listing](https://massivebookshop.com/products/9781633434882)].

**Educative** [primary, /unlimited]: AI mock interviews (coding and system design), personalised
interview prep, an AI tutor, Grokking courses; claims "3.1 million developers".

**Coursera + Udemy**: merger closed **11 May 2026** (0.8 Coursera shares per Udemy share)
[primary, [Coursera 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001651562/000165156226000072/cour-20260930.htm)];
reported 290M+ learners and ~$115M expected cost synergies [secondary,
[Built In](https://builtin.com/articles/udemy-coursera-combination-20260603)]. Scale in courses,
not in deep reading of one book.

**What the group can't do:** books from other publishers in one place; figures you can play with;
a schedule; files the reader owns. **Risk:** they own the text and can put a loop inside it. **Our
stance:** any publisher, local; and packs made *with* a publisher or author, not against them.

## 4. Free AI tutors: give "explain this" away

**Gemini Notebook** (NotebookLM until **16 July 2026**) [primary,
[Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/notebooklm-gemini-notebook/)]:
source-grounded answers, audio overviews; flashcards and quizzes since September 2025, saved
progress and mastery tracking (April 2026), editable flashcards (June 2026) [secondary,
[April update](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/1391/notebooklm-april-2026-update-auto-label-flashcards),
[flashcards](https://bibigpt.co/features/notebooklm-flashcards-quizzes-explained)]. Paid quota only
through Google AI plans [secondary]. The previous research cited ~17M monthly users (mid-2026); a
headline cites ~30M users, unverified.

**ChatGPT Study Mode** (July 2025): Socratic questions, quizzes, for logged-in Free, Plus, Pro and
Team users [secondary,
[Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/openai-introduces-study-mode-chatgpt-125072901816_1.html)].

**Claude Code output styles**: "Explanatory" adds insights while it works; "Learning" also asks
you to write parts of the code (`TODO(human)`) [primary,
[Claude Code docs](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/output-styles)]. *Learning inside the coding
agent, for the codebase in front of you, not for a book.*

**Kindle "Ask this Book"**: announced 11 December 2025 for the US iOS app; on some 2026 Kindles
with software 5.20.2; Android expected by end of 2026; English only; always on, no author opt-out,
which the Authors Guild objected to [secondary,
[MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/12/15/kindle-app-ios-gains-in-book-ai-chatbot/),
[TechRadar](https://techradar.com/tablets/ereaders/amazons-ask-this-book-feature-is-finally-rolling-out-to-kindles-but-certain-models-and-regions-may-be-excluded);
primary, [Authors Guild](https://authorsguild.org/news/statement-on-amazon-kindle-ask-this-book-ai-feature/)].

**What the group can't do:** keep a schedule across months tied to page anchors; draw the system
as something you can scrub; let the learner keep everything as files. **Risk:** "good enough" for
most people; this is risk #1 in the main document. **Our stance:** never compete on explaining;
compete on the loop and on what's left three weeks later.

## 5. Spaced repetition and courses with review built in

**Anki**: free, the standard; FSRS scheduling (which we also use). Card-making is manual.

**RemNote**: notes plus spaced repetition; Pro $8/month, Pro with AI $18/month (AI flashcards,
quizzes and summaries from PDFs) [secondary,
[Costbench](https://costbench.com/software/note-taking/remnote/)]. *The closest single product to
our reader-plus-cards, cloud-hosted, without figures.*

**Execute Program** (Gary Bernhardt): interactive courses (TypeScript, SQL, regexes…) with
automatic spaced repetition, **$39/month**, 16 free lessons [secondary, quoting the site,
[review](https://code.brettchalupa.com/execute-program-review)]. *Proof that developers pay for
spaced repetition, on the vendor's own curriculum.*

**Boot.dev**: gamified backend courses (Python, Go), **$59/month or $399/year** [secondary];
bootstrapped, revenue put at $1.3M (2024) by Latka or $5.7M by another tracker, 850k+ users and
25k+ paying claimed [secondary, [Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/boot.dev/funding),
[research page](https://onepage-research.sliplane.app/products/boot-dev)]. *The best comparable for
the size of a developer-learning business run by a small team.*

**Readwise Reader**: read-later with Ghostreader AI, $9.99/month billed yearly, no free tier
[secondary]. Highlights review, not understanding.

## 6. Career growth: same buyer, different job

**Taro** (YC S22): Q&A and courses from senior engineers on onboarding and promotion; "Premium"
membership; **1,250 paying members** at the end of 2023; members' learning budgets often pay
[primary, [2023 review](https://blog.jointaro.com/taros-2023-year-in-review),
[L&D page](https://www.jointaro.com/learninganddevelopment/taro)]. **The Pragmatic Engineer**:
~1.1M subscribers, $150/year paid [secondary]. *They own the promotion conversation; neither teaches
the systems themselves.*

## 7. Onboarding and context tools (the team tier, later)

**Unblocked** (context engine for engineering teams), **Glean** (enterprise search with
engineering prompting), **Swimm** (code-linked docs), **DeepWiki** (free wikis for 50,000+ public
repos) [secondary, [Unblocked comparison](https://getunblocked.com/blog/best-developer-onboarding-tools/index.md),
a competitor; [Falconer guide](https://falconer.com/guides/developer-onboarding-tools),
[SSOJet](https://ssojet.com/blog/ai-codebase-understanding-tools)]. Atlan launched a "Context
Engineering Studio" for enterprise data in 2026 [primary,
[Atlan](https://atlan.com/snowflake-summit-2026-announcements/)]. For individuals, the free Claudian
plugin runs Claude Code or Codex inside an Obsidian vault (528k downloads) [primary,
[Obsidian community](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/realclaudian)].

*These tools answer questions about company knowledge; none makes a new hire able to explain the
system. That is the team tier's angle: onboarding that ends in understanding, with the same vault
as context for the team's agents. It is crowded and enterprise-sold, so it comes after the
individual product works.*

## 8. Threat ranking

| Threat | Likelihood (12 months) | Impact | Our response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free tutors are "good enough" | High | High | Compete on the loop and what remains in three weeks; kill if week-1 return < 15% |
| Interview platforms add explain-back and review | Medium-high (Hello Interview is close) | High | Partner; position as "understand the book" beside their question banks |
| Publishers put a loop inside their books | Medium | High | Any publisher, local; packs with authors |
| Gemini Notebook adds figures of systems | Low-medium | Medium | Packs (checked figures per book) and local files |
| A clone of our open-source app | Low | Low-medium | AGPL; the packs and the learner's history are the moat, not the code |
